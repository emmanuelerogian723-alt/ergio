About Us

Lagos Bistro

Lagos Bistro is Victoria Island, Lagos's premier restaurant, built on a foundation of excellence and deep roots in the Nigerian community. We combine world-class standards with an authentic local touch — ensuring every client receives an experience that truly stands out. Lagos Bistro serves authentic Nigerian and continental cuisine with lunch specials, fine dining dinners, and event catering services. Our mission is to provide exceptional dining experiences that showcase the rich flavors of Africa. We take pride in our commitment to quality, hospitality, and community.

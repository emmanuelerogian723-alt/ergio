Welcome to ERGIO
Your AI Business Operating System. I'll show you around in 30 seconds — let's get you oriented so you can start building immediately.
The Conductor
This is your AI command center. Ask it to find leads, build websites, send outreach, or run any business task. It has 10 engines, 25 MCP servers, and 6 agent frameworks at its disposal. Just type what you need.
Website Builder
Describe your business and ERGIO generates a complete website with motion graphics, SEO, and live preview. Pick from 16 business types, 8 color themes, and 3 animation styles. Deploy with one click.
Leads CRM + Outreach
Scan for potential clients, score leads, and send personalized outreach via email or WhatsApp. All powered by the AI discovery engine. Your leads sync with Supabase automatically.
MCP, Plugins & Integrations
Connect 25 MCP servers (Supabase, Stripe, GitHub, Groq, OpenAI, and more) and 30 plugins. Manage API keys, toggle connections, and extend ERGIO's capabilities without writing code.
Cloud Console & New Tabs
Monitor your infrastructure, AI employees, missions, memory, scheduler, health, and security — all in one place. Everything syncs with your actual connections and data in real-time.
Billing & Plans
Start free, upgrade when ready. Pay with Paystack (Naira) or Stripe. Plans from Free to Enterprise (₦75k/mo). All payments are secured with 256-bit SSL.
You're All Set
That's the tour! Start by opening the Conductor and asking it to do something — like "find 10 potential clients in Lagos" or "build a website for a gym." You can always replay this tour from the help button.
